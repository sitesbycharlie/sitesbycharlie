Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

Who I am

This website is run by me, trading as Sites By Charlie, a one-person web design service based in Dublin, Ireland. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact me at sitesbycharlie@gmail.com.

What data I collect

I only collect personal data that you voluntarily provide through the contact form on this website. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Which plan you're interested in, if you select one
  • Any message or project details you choose to share

I don't collect any data automatically. This website uses no tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or any other passive data collection beyond standard Google Analytics page-view tracking.

How I use your data

The information you submit via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about your project. I will never use your details for unsolicited marketing, and I will never sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

How your data is stored

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service. Formspree stores your submission on their servers in order to deliver it to me. You can read Formspree's own privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

I don't operate my own database or server-side storage of your personal information.

Cookies

This website does not set any first-party cookies of its own. Formspree may set a functional cookie in order to process contact form submissions, this is strictly necessary for the form to operate and does not track you across other websites. Google Analytics may also set cookies to measure anonymous page-view traffic.

Your rights under GDPR

As someone based in or dealing with businesses in Ireland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to access, you can request a copy of any personal data I hold about you
  • The right to erasure, you can ask me to delete your personal data
  • The right to rectification, you can ask me to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to object, you can object to how I process your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at sitesbycharlie@gmail.com and I will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. I recommend checking back occasionally if you have an ongoing relationship with me.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to your personal data, please get in touch: sitesbycharlie@gmail.com
